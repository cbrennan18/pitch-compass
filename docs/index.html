Meet the one pitch that faces the way you chose — a real, named ground. Its name, size and bearing are on the field. It agrees with you. But does Ireland?

2,707 committees answered before you. Here is every one of their answers, each pitch at its true shape, size and bearing. Together they draw the island unaided. Tap any pitch.

How we did this

Placeholder six-sentence method summary. (1) Every pitch comes from OpenStreetMap; (2) the signal filter keeps 2,707 GAA-tagged pitches by shape and size; (3) a pitch's bearing is the long axis of its minimum-area rotated rectangle; (4) orientation is axial (a line, not a direction), so every statistic folds bearings onto a half-circle; (5) county assignment is ray-cast point-in-polygon against the boundary files; (6) every number on this page is regenerated from the frozen exploration log, nothing hand-typed.

The method is open and the repository is public. Read the frozen findings and reproduce every figure from the scripts:

  • FINDINGS.md — the frozen exploration log
  • Source repository
  • Pitch data © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0 — openstreetmap.org/copyright
  • County boundaries (26): Tailte Éireann, Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
  • County boundaries (6): Ordnance Survey of Northern Ireland, UK Open Government Licence
  • The coastline is derived from those same county boundary files — no separate download.
